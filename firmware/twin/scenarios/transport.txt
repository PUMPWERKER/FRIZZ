# The transport knob through a standstill (#174): a loop slowed in semitones to 1/16x, 2 detents
# more to a standstill (the loop silent, both transport LEDs red), a morph to the blank scene
# landing meanwhile on the beat that runs on at 50 BPM, then 4 detents on into reverse, and the
# press back to 1x. The filter and the crusher latched by hand; nothing is saved. (turn: 5 is
# the transport, 4 knob 1; detents play out 8 ms each)
input sine 220 0.3
at 6000
tap KEY_28
wait 1500
tap KEY_28
wait 500
input off
down KEY_5
wait 80
tap KEY_26
up KEY_5
wait 200
down KEY_4
wait 80
tap KEY_26
up KEY_4
wait 500
turn 5 -96
wait 4000
# 1/16x forward: the forward LED red-orange, the reverse one red
expect led pth 6 fd1600
expect led pth 5 d10000
turn 5 -2
wait 3000
# a standstill: both red
expect led pth 5 fd0000
expect led pth 6 fd0000
# SHIFT + the blank scene, let go: lands on a bar line within 2 bars at 50 BPM (9.6 s)
down KEY_26
wait 80
tap KEY_18
wait 80
up KEY_26
wait 10000
# landed: the blank scene active, the filter and crusher off; still standing
expect led smt 2 fcfcfc
expect led smt 20 240c14
expect led smt 21 241408
expect led pth 5 fd0000
expect led pth 6 fd0000
# 4 detents on: 1/16x reverse
turn 5 -4
wait 3000
expect led pth 5 fd1600
expect led pth 6 d10000
# the press: 1x forward
tap ENC_5_SW
wait 1500
expect led pth 6 79f200
expect led pth 5 000000
