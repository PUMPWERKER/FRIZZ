# The delay and the reverb (#172) on a sine: each latched at its defaults, heard at once; page
# 2's knob 4, its Level, down and back up to 0dB; page 1's knob 4, its Ducking, up; the
# reverb's pre-delay up and back to 0, where it switches off (#167); then off, the tails ringing
# out. Over MIDI, CC 21 on the delay's channel (12) sets its Level, CC 17 its Ducking. Nothing
# is saved. (turn: 4 is knob 1, 1-3 knobs 2-4)
input sine 220 0.3
at 6000
# the delay latched at its defaults
down KEY_12
wait 80
tap KEY_26
up KEY_12
wait 1500
# page 2: Level down 12dB and back
tap ENC_4_SW
wait 300
turn 3 -25
wait 1000
turn 3 25
wait 1000
# page 1: Ducking up and back off
tap ENC_4_SW
wait 300
turn 3 50
wait 1500
turn 3 -50
wait 500
# over MIDI: Level and Ducking on its channel
midi BB 15 40
midi BB 11 60
wait 1000
midi BB 15 60
midi BB 11 00
wait 500
# page 1: random at its centre, white; Ducking at 0, the delay's first colour
expect led pth 3 fdfdfd
expect led pth 4 00fd00
# the reverb latched at its defaults, the delay into it
down KEY_13
wait 80
tap KEY_26
up KEY_13
wait 1500
# page 2: pre-delay up to 250ms, Level down and back; the pre-delay back to 0
tap ENC_4_SW
wait 300
turn 1 100
wait 1500
turn 3 -25
wait 1000
turn 3 25
wait 500
turn 1 -100
wait 1500
# page 2: freeze and the pre-delay at 0, Band at its centre
expect led pth 1 00fd84
expect led pth 2 00fd84
expect led pth 3 fdfdfd
# both off, SHIFT held (#456): the tails ring out
down KEY_26
wait 60
tap KEY_12
wait 100
tap KEY_13
up KEY_26
wait 4000
