# The frequency shifter (#306), the phaser's second mode on the 7th white key: SHIFT + KEY_7,
# then KEY_7 again in the same hold, switches it and leaves the latch as it was (#456), the key
# ice blue and knob 4 (the direction) white in the middle. Latched on a sine against a MIDI clock: all wet, a barber pole (a few
# Hz, feedback up), then about 90 Hz, the direction down and up, page 2's stereo up, the shift
# synced (knob 1 green); then back to the phaser the same way, and off. Nothing is saved.
# (turn: 4 is knob 1, 1-3 knobs 2-4)
input sine 220 0.3
clock 120
at 6000
down KEY_26
wait 60
tap KEY_7
wait 100
tap KEY_7
wait 100
up KEY_26
wait 500
expect led smt 18 101424
expect led pth 4 fdfdfd
# latched
down KEY_7
wait 80
tap KEY_26
up KEY_7
wait 200
# all wet (depth up), feedback at the top
turn 2 100
wait 600
turn 1 60 130
wait 7920
# the shift down from 10 Hz to about 1.3 Hz: a barber pole
turn 4 -11 130
wait 5342
# about 90 Hz
turn 4 23 130
wait 4806
# the direction down, then up: knob 4's LED blue, then orange
turn 3 -50
wait 1500
expect led pth 4 0000fd
turn 3 100
wait 1500
expect led pth 4 fd8f37
# page 2: stereo up, the right channel the other way, then back
tap ENC_4_SW
wait 300
turn 1 100
wait 1500
tap ENC_4_SW
wait 300
# synced: the first division, 1/16, green
turn 4 13 130
wait 3586
expect led pth 1 00fd00
# back to the phaser: blue, knob 4 the phaser's centre
down KEY_26
wait 60
tap KEY_7
wait 100
tap KEY_7
wait 100
up KEY_26
wait 500
expect led pth 4 fd8f37
# off again, the key, then SHIFT: its key dim blue
down KEY_7
wait 80
tap KEY_26
up KEY_7
wait 1000
expect led smt 18 000024
