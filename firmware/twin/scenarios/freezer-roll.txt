# The freezer's roll (#173) on a sine against a MIDI clock: latched at 1/4 bar, the roll turned
# right a step a beat, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8 and 1/16 of the length, back to off in one move; let go
# with the roll on 1/4, and latched again: that capture starts on 1/4. Nothing is saved.
# (turn: 4 is knob 1, 1-3 knobs 2-4; one step per 3 detents)
input sine 220 0.3
clock 120
at 6000
# latched, its length from 1 bar to 1/4
down KEY_1
wait 80
tap KEY_26
up KEY_1
wait 200
turn 4 -9
wait 1000
# the roll a step a beat: each takes at the next repeat's start
turn 2 3
wait 500
turn 2 3
wait 500
turn 2 3
wait 500
turn 2 3
wait 1000
# length 1/4, the roll on 1/16, its last colour
expect led pth 1 3779fd
expect led pth 3 00d1fd
# back to off in one move: the whole 1/4 again
turn 2 -12
wait 1500
# on 1/4, let go, latched again: the new capture repeats a quarter of the length
turn 2 6
wait 300
tap KEY_1
wait 600
down KEY_1
wait 80
tap KEY_26
up KEY_1
wait 2000
# the roll on 1/4
expect led pth 3 426efd
# off again
turn 2 -6
wait 300
tap KEY_1
wait 1000
