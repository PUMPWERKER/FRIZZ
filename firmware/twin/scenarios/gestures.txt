# The scene gestures (#175), SHIFT held from the first scene key: the filter latched and closed,
# saved to slot 1 (the lower G#, KEY_19; the card isn't written on the device, PERSIST 0), the
# blank scene (the lower F#, KEY_18) recalled. Then a build-up (SHIFT + slot 1, then the blank
# scene: the filter closes over the bar, the blank scene drops on the line), a drop with 2
# detents turned while pressing (the press wins, no crossfader), and a morph replaced by another
# in a later hold (it ends on the new one). (turn: 4 is knob 1, 5 the transport)

wait 500

tap KEY_19
