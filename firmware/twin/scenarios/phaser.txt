# The phaser (#68) on the 7th white key: latched on a sine against a MIDI clock, depth, feedback
# and centre up; its rate turned from free into the synced half, where knob 1's LED turns from
# the phaser's colours to green, SHIFT + turn stepping through the divisions and back into the
# free half; page 2's stereo up (#117); then off. Nothing is saved. (turn: 4 is knob 1, 1-3 knobs
# 2-4)
input sine 220 0.3
clock 120
at 6000
# latched: its key lit, blue
down KEY_7
wait 80
tap KEY_26
up KEY_7
wait 200
# its knobs at their defaults: rate .25, between blue and orange; feedback .4; depth blue;
# centre .5
expect led pth 1 79429a
expect led pth 2 6337b0
expect led pth 3 0000fd
expect led pth 4 79429a
# depth, feedback up, the centre up an octave
turn 3 17 130
wait 2674
turn 1 50 130
wait 6600
turn 2 100
wait 1000
expect led pth 2 dc844d
expect led pth 3 fd8f37
expect led pth 4 a56379
# rate (.7 Hz, at .25) up to the centre: the first division, 1/16, green
turn 4 25 130
wait 5050
expect led pth 1 00fd00
# fine turns step a division per 3 detents: 1/8, a little towards yellow
turn 4 3
wait 2000
expect led pth 1 21f200
# SHIFT + turn: the next division each detent, 1/2, halfway to yellow
down KEY_26
wait 50
turn 4 2
wait 100
up KEY_26
wait 2000
expect led pth 1 79f200
# and back down past 1/16 into the free half: on the 10% steps, at .4, nearly orange
down KEY_26
wait 50
turn 4 -4
wait 100
up KEY_26
wait 2000
expect led pth 1 c67963
# page 2: stereo up, the right LFO behind, then back
tap ENC_4_SW
wait 300
turn 1 100
wait 1500
turn 1 -100
wait 1500
tap ENC_4_SW
wait 300
# off again: its key dim
down KEY_7
wait 80
up KEY_7
wait 1000
expect led smt 18 000024
