# The phaser (#68) on the 11th white key: latched on a sine against a MIDI clock, depth, feedback
# and stereo up; its rate turned from free into the synced half, where knob 1's LED turns from
# the phaser's colours to green, SHIFT + turn stepping through the divisions and back into the
# free half; page 2's centre swept; then off. Nothing is saved. (turn: 4 is knob 1, 1-3 knobs
# 2-4)
input sine 220 0.3
clock 120
at 6000
# latched: its key lit, azure
down KEY_11
wait 80
tap KEY_26
up KEY_11
wait 200
# its knobs at their defaults: rate .25, between azure and magenta; feedback .4; depth and stereo azure
expect led pth 1 7937fd
expect led pth 2 6342fd
expect led pth 3 006efd
expect led pth 4 006efd
# depth, feedback, stereo up
turn 3 60
wait 600
turn 1 50
wait 500
turn 2 100
wait 1000
expect led pth 2 dc0bfd
expect led pth 3 fd00fd
expect led pth 4 8f2cfd
# rate (.7 Hz, at .25) up to the centre: the first division, 1/16, green
turn 4 25
wait 2000
expect led pth 1 00fd00
# fine turns step a division per 3 detents: 1/8, a little towards yellow
turn 4 3
wait 2000
expect led pth 1 21f200
# SHIFT + turn: the next division each detent, 1/2, halfway to yellow
down KEY_26
wait 50
turn 4 2
wait 100
up KEY_26
wait 2000
expect led pth 1 79f200
# and back down past 1/16 into the free half: on the 10% steps, at .4, nearly magenta
down KEY_26
wait 50
turn 4 -4
wait 100
up KEY_26
wait 2000
expect led pth 1 c616fd
# page 2: the centre swept up two octaves and back
tap ENC_4_SW
wait 300
turn 1 33
wait 1500
turn 1 -33
wait 1500
tap ENC_4_SW
wait 300
# off again: its key dim
down KEY_11
wait 80
up KEY_11
wait 1000
expect led smt 14 001024
